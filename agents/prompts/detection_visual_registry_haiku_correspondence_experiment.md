## Cross-view correspondence audit for Haiku

Before locking commercial objects, compare every visually separate depiction
that could be a complete product with the other complete-product depictions.
`related_groups` is a correspondence ledger, not optional navigation text.
Every relation must name a concrete `match_basis` visible in both depictions.
Empty `related_groups` is an explicit finding that no alternate-view
correspondence is visually supportable after this comparison.

Two depictions may be alternate views even when their crop, camera angle,
orientation or amount of visible detail differs. Relate them only when the
`match_basis` identifies a distinctive physical pattern visible in both, such
as the same carcass, worktop, unique panel sequence, unusual silhouette,
colour arrangement, mounting structure or matching dimension chain. Do not
relate them merely because they share a page, room, project, finish, generic
metal profile, generic rail or proximity.

For every related pair, either put both groups in the same commercial object or
state in `boundary_basis` the specific incompatible envelope, function or
structure that proves they are different products. A change of view, crop or
rendering angle is not such proof. Do not split two linked views into separate
objects merely because each view is a complete-looking drawing.
