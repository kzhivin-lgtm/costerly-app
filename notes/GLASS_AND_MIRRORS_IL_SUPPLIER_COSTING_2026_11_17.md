## Proposed calculation

- Name: **Glass & Mirrors**. Route ready-to-fit pieces as purchased components. Use the saved Parts Check with both Block and Common ownership. Preserve a single costing owner per part. Keep glass box bonding and fixing the parts into furniture separate; when buying a finished box, its quote replaces both component fabrication and bonding.
- Parts emits quantity, geometry, material family, thickness, finish and treatment evidence if present. Group equal parts for display and vendor enquiry without losing individual dimensions, ownership or the source/derived/assumed distinction. Use ordinary clear as the optical default for transparent glass. Use 5 mm standard silver as an estimating default for a frameless mirror with missing thickness, never as a fabrication instruction. Honor explicit thickness, heat treatment and safety backing. A role-based thickness or safety assumption must stay visible, and special applications require confirmation.
- Default edge: simple straight, smooth safe finish. Where a supplier distinguishes the grades, use a small eased edge (arris) when concealed and flat polish when exposed; when visibility is unclear, budget the perimeter at the plain finish. No decorative bevel is inferred. For each edge, store length and the required profile, angle and bevel width if specified. A decorative face bevel and a 45° miter for joining glass are different operations; do not charge standard polish on the same edge unless explicitly a separate step in the accepted quote.
- Calculate finished area per piece and meters per specified edge profile. Store outline/cut geometry separately; use part count, linear cut meters or sheet rate strictly according to the quote. In a straight rectangular package, no separate estimate of cut travel is needed. For special contours, holes, notches, cutouts or miters, price per supplied operations or request a quote. All these operations precede tempering.
- Resolve the complete price for each part or declared batch. Use a matching explicit supplier quote first, then matching company tariff, then Israeli public reference with its exact scope, constraints and VAT basis. A bundle can cover stock, cut, edge and tempering under one amount; expose the inclusions without charging them again as Materials or in Labor. A supplier can instead quote itemized raw stock (finished area or full purchased sheets), cut, edge and tempering. Charge full-sheet waste or sheet counts only if the vendor sells that way. Apply per-piece, batch or order minimum only at the level stated by that vendor. If scope or price is incomplete, retain the priced subtotal separately and list the remaining pieces or operations requiring a quote. Do not fill with an unlabelled zero or substitute another thickness.
- Use a batch enquiry for repeated small glass panels outside the retail product scope or where a batch quote is appropriate. Ask for net price for the full lot, tempering, basic edge scope, minimum order and size, and separate ₪ per metre for optional bevel/miter if offered. Request glass box bonding separately as an option. Delivery and on-site installation stay separate.
- Suggested card presentation: item count, finished area, purchased subtotal, what is included and one actionable remainder if quote coverage is incomplete. Keep measurements and per-edge data available for an expanded audit. Price-source freshness and assumptions belong with the tariff/line.

## Workshop budget model, pending fresh price calibration

Owner decision: do not use the 2017 Orchard prices in either Israel Reference or Assumption Fallback. Preserve that source only as evidence that the operations can be quoted separately. The table and replay below are historical to the first experiment; do not use them as live rates. Recalibrate the rates influenced by Orchard, including straight cutting and glass, polishing and the Extra Clear multiplier, against newer evidence before adoption.

Estimate the finished made-to-size component. Use actual finished area in m², actual finished edge length in m, and the physical piece count. Include glass and cutting; add edge finishing only for the relevant edges and tempering only when specified or required for the application. A comparable finished-part supplier quote replaces the estimate for all operations it includes. Figures below record the previous offline experiment in ILS excluding VAT. Exclude delivery, fitting, box bonding and on-site work. Mirrors keep a separate price path and never inherit glass tempering rates.

| 4 | 140 | 70 |
| 5 | 155 | 75 |
| 6 | 170 | 85 |
| 8 | 210 | 110 |

For each piece, add `actual_area × (glass_and_cut × grade_multiplier + tempering_if_required) + polished_edge_m × 20 + arrised_edge_m × 8 + 8`. Multiply by physical quantity, then sum across the purchase order. Apply `max(80, order_subtotal)` once per actual workshop order, including if several objects or thicknesses share that order. For an empty order there is no charge. Per-piece handling covers sorting and handling of individual pieces; the area rate already includes straight cutting. Never inflate the area to 0.65 m² or apply a minimum separately to each part. Round currency only after summation for display.

The replay applied a 1.5 Extra Clear multiplier only to glass and straight cutting. It used flat polished edges where visible or unspecified, and arrising on confirmed hidden edges where the assembly accepts it. Price each edge once. An explicit 45-degree bevel, formed shape, holes or unsupported stock require a supplier override or quote flag instead of silently substituting a rectangular polished pane. Price output does not confirm that a workshop can temper a particular geometry. Store supplier overrides against the same scope, so a bundled quote that includes tempering or edge finish is never charged those operations again in Materials or Labor.
