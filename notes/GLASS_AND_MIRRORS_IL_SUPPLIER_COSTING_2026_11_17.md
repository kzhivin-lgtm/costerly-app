# Glass & Mirrors: Israeli supplier costing

Research and offline replay, 2026-11-17. No production calculation changed.

## Public references

[Agan Barzel tempered cut-to-size glass](https://aganbarzel.com/products/%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A1%D7%9E%D7%AA) sells finished rectangular pieces with polished edges and tempering. The public calculator configuration and rendered prices agree: glass rates by thickness, extra-clear surcharge, and a 0.65 m² billing floor per piece. Geometry is checked separately: one side 300–2000 mm and the other 50–1100 mm (rotate if orientation is immaterial). The regular product has no special holes, cutouts or corners. Shipping is separate.

| 4 / 5 / 6 mm | 320 | 460 | 208 / 299 |

These are the profile's nominal rates. The site also adds finished area × 0.001 × base thickness rate: the 10 mm result shown for exactly 1 m² is ₪551 or ₪691. Keep the cents internally and round only for presentation. Direct browser checks also reproduced 4 mm clear/extra clear at 800 × 400 mm: ₪208/299 shown. The extra-clear premium is specific to this profile, not a universal raw glass surcharge. Ordinary clear float has a green cast; extra-clear float has reduced iron and much less color ([NSG/Pilkington Optiwhite](https://www.pilkington.com/en-us/united-states-and-canada/architectural-and-technical-glass/product-categories/enhanced-visibility/pilkington-optiwhite)).

[Agan Barzel frameless mirror](https://aganbarzel.com/products/frameless) sells a 5 mm cut-to-size silver mirror with full diamond-polished perimeter at max(₪385 × area in m², ₪220), including VAT and assuming pickup and direct mounting without paid options. One side 200–2000 mm and the other 100–1100 mm. No holes or cutouts. Product description says Belgian crystal; it gives no basis for an automatic low-iron upgrade. [Tax Authority standard VAT rate](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/vat-history): divide explicitly VAT-inclusive prices by 1.18 for net cost.

## Proposed calculation

- Name: **Glass & Mirrors**. Route ready-to-fit pieces as purchased components. Use the saved Parts Check with both Block and Common ownership. Preserve a single costing owner per part. Keep glass box bonding and fixing the parts into furniture separate; when buying a finished box, its quote replaces both component fabrication and bonding.
- Parts emits quantity, geometry, material family, thickness, finish and treatment evidence if present. Group equal parts for display and vendor enquiry without losing individual dimensions, ownership or the source/derived/assumed distinction. Use ordinary clear as the optical default for transparent glass. Use 5 mm standard silver as an estimating default for a frameless mirror with missing thickness, never as a fabrication instruction. Honor explicit thickness, heat treatment and safety backing. A role-based thickness or safety assumption must stay visible, and special applications require confirmation.
- Default edge: simple straight, smooth safe finish. Where a supplier distinguishes the grades, use a small eased edge (arris) when concealed and flat polish when exposed; when visibility is unclear, budget the perimeter at the plain finish. No decorative bevel is inferred. For each edge, store length and the required profile, angle and bevel width if specified. A decorative face bevel and a 45° miter for joining glass are different operations; do not charge standard polish on the same edge unless explicitly a separate step in the accepted quote.
- Calculate finished area per piece and meters per specified edge profile. Store outline/cut geometry separately; use part count, linear cut meters or sheet rate strictly according to the quote. In a straight rectangular package, no separate estimate of cut travel is needed. For special contours, holes, notches, cutouts or miters, price per supplied operations or request a quote. All these operations precede tempering.
- Resolve the complete price for each part or declared batch. Use a matching explicit supplier quote first, then matching company tariff, then Israeli public reference with its exact scope, constraints and VAT basis. A bundle can cover stock, cut, edge and tempering under one amount; expose the inclusions without charging them again as Materials or in Labor. A supplier can instead quote itemized raw stock (finished area or full purchased sheets), cut, edge and tempering. Charge full-sheet waste or sheet counts only if the vendor sells that way. Apply per-piece, batch or order minimum only at the level stated by that vendor. If scope or price is incomplete, retain the priced subtotal separately and list the remaining pieces or operations requiring a quote. Do not fill with an unlabelled zero or substitute another thickness.
- Use a batch enquiry for repeated small glass panels outside the retail product scope or where a batch quote is appropriate. Ask for net price for the full lot, tempering, basic edge scope, minimum order and size, and separate ₪ per metre for optional bevel/miter if offered. Request glass box bonding separately as an option. Delivery and on-site installation stay separate.
- Suggested card presentation: item count, finished area, purchased subtotal, what is included and one actionable remainder if quote coverage is incomplete. Keep measurements and per-edge data available for an expanded audit. Price-source freshness and assumptions belong with the tariff/line.

## Follow-up: recorded offers and alternatives

- Live Israel Reference now has 11 `fabricated_component` candidate offers and two source records for the public Agan Barzel calculators. Each offer includes the exact 1000 × 1000 mm price, formula inputs, gross minimum, admissible size range, VAT and included operations. No normalized linear baseline was written because pricing varies with finished piece size. Existing pricing and material defaults are unaffected. The idempotent seed is `tools/seed_israel_glass_mirror_offers.py`, with captured calculator settings in `notes/evidence/glass-mirrors-il/agan-barzel-2026-11-17.json`. Live readback matched all 11 offers and both sources.
- Example: ten 400 × 250 mm ordinary 4 mm panels are 1 m² of physical area but 6.5 m² of minimum billing in the Agan online formula; about ₪2080 with VAT before shipping, excluding any negotiated price for a batch. The 0.65 m² minimum affects billing; the 300 × 50 mm minimum affects whether the online dimensions are accepted at all.
- [B&A Glass](https://baglass.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A4%D7%99-%D7%96%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A4%D7%99-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%93%D7%94-%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A8/) supplies custom glass shelves to businesses, including wholesale, but gives price factors rather than published rates. [Lior Alfasi Glass Warehouse](https://www.b144.co.il/b144_sip/401D0D1A4370655D4F10001B4C/) advertises contractor and wholesale prices for cut-to-size glass and mirrors without publishing those prices or its minimum. GL Glass has cutting, polishing and separate furnace capability. The [Ministry of Defense construction item register](https://www.online.mod.gov.il/Online2016/Pages/General/SapakInfo/BinuyTree.aspx?id=12.00.00.0000) explicitly lists perimeter diamond-polishing as a separate item (12.62.01.6010), without a usable open price.
- For a simple production rate card use raw/substrate price per m² or actual sheet, cut per piece or running metre as quoted, one edge profile per treated running metre, and tempering per m² or per batch. An explicit per-piece handling charge or per-batch minimum is preferable to assuming a universal small-part factor. A bundled written quote supersedes the individual lines it covers. To pin down the minimum and the separate rates, ask for 1 and 10 identical 400 × 250 mm panels and for the whole saved Adda batch. Request clear and extra-clear 4 mm, collection only, with separate material, cutting, edge finishing, tempering and minimum when available. No request was sent.
