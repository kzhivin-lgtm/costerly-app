A separate Data Processing Agreement may be made available for customers that require one. No separate DPA checkbox applies to ordinary users.

Information may be shared with subprocessors that support hosting, databases, authentication, payments, monitoring, communications, support and AI processing. Costerly AI does not use Customer Content to train its own general-purpose AI models. OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and any other AI provider engaged for the service must not use Customer Content to train or improve their general-purpose models. Customer Content is transmitted to AI providers only to perform requested service functions.

Such copies remain protected and are not restored to active use except for continuity, security or legal necessity.
